# roadmap

1. **pricing test screen.** After Sunday's note, three monthly price points (for example ₹199, ₹399, ₹699) shown as plain choices; the pick stays on the device and is shown back to the tester, so Ananya can log it by hand.
2. **kid-side check-in.** The two taps Meera makes (effort, "i can figure this out") plus a CEI-II short form for curiosity, with an assent screen written for a twelve-year-old and a score card that never says "correct", never ranks.
3. **weekly home-experiment card.** Sunday's note gains one kitchen or balcony experiment drawn from the week's questions (the pressure cooker whistle, the paper boat).
4. **share the dinner question.** A share button that sends only the dinner question text (never the child's name or questions) to WhatsApp, with a clear note of what leaves the device.
5. **second sample child.** A younger child (Aarav, 7) so parents of 6 to 8 year olds see a week that sounds like their home, plus a Kannada and Hindi read-through by native-speaking parents.
